Student finds nine million euros behind cellar wall

Sint-Gillis-bij-Dendermonde, East Flanders, Belgium · 14 Aug 2026

Kobe, eighteen, was laying a sewage pipe for the firm De Brand when part of a foundation came away and a chest appeared: more than four thousand coins and forty gold bars. The crew briefly discussed keeping it. Prosecutors do not know whose it is. Police have warned off treasure hunters.

The Bar Caffè Basile opened in 1888, after Aniello Basile went to Naples to learn gelato, and closed in January after a hundred and thirty-eight years. Residents have reopened it as a space they run themselves: a drink, cards, board games, and somewhere to sit.

On the third Thursday of August the village climbs the hill to Ömer Dede’s grave, sacrifices about twenty goats and cooks them in seven or eight cauldrons. Two thousand people came this year, some from Diyarbakır. In the years it was skipped, they say, there was drought and locusts.

More than a hundred torches three metres high, bound into the shape of bamboo shoots, are lit at once along the main street, with smaller stacks outside each house. The festival has run for over four hundred and fifty years, to keep Mount Fuji from erupting.

16

The guga hunt is refused a licence

Ness, Isle of Lewis, Scotland · 3 Aug 2026

Every August since at least the fifteenth century, ten men from Ness have sailed forty miles north to Sula Sgeir, a bare rock in the Atlantic, and spent a fortnight taking young gannets — guga — from the cliffs, salting them and bringing them home. The hunt has run under licence for decades, capped at two thousand birds. This year NatureScot refused, for the first time in recorded history. Its board commissioned a new population model after a seventeen per cent decline across the north-east Atlantic gannet population, and concluded that harm was possible even at five hundred birds. The refusal does not bar future applications. What has changed on the island is the volume: residents describe months of online abuse, and one told a reporter the community had been drowned out, silenced and shouted at.

Ten men, a bare Atlantic rock, and two weeks of salt barrels.

STV News·news.stv.tv
